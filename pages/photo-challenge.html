Our Mission

After 30 years of exploring Kenya, we believe the true essence of this land is captured in fleeting moments — seen by those who live and breathe it. 61 Kingdom launches this ongoing showcase to create a window for Africa lovers: collecting and celebrating the most authentic and moving moments of Kenya through your unique perspectives.

Our Vision

We are committed to promoting these vibrant, soul-stirring works to a wider stage — allowing the world to rediscover the beauty of Kenya through your eyes. Every submission is a story, and every story deserves to be seen. Join us in building the most authentic visual record of Kenya in existence.

How it works

Participation Rules

01

Open to Everyone

Inclusivity is at the heart of this showcase. Whether you are a local driver, a professional guide, or a passing traveller — whether you use a smartphone or a DSLR — your lens is welcome here. No gatekeeping, no barriers.

02

Vision Over Technique

This is not a technical contest. We value stories, emotions, and cultural insights over camera settings and post-processing skill. What moves us matters far more than what your aperture was set to. Authenticity wins every time.

03

One Photo Per Entry

Submit your single strongest image. Include a short photo story — the context, the moment, what you felt when you captured it. Your words give the image its full meaning and help our judges connect with your perspective.

04

Kenya-Focused Subjects

All submissions must capture something of Kenya — its wildlife, landscapes, people, culture, traditions, or daily life. The image should tell a story that is unmistakably rooted in this extraordinary land and its communities.

Ownership & respect

Your Work,
Your Rights

We believe photographers should always retain ownership of their creative work. Our relationship with every contributor is built on transparency, respect, and a shared love for Kenya's beauty.

Every image submitted is treated with care — never sold, never exploited, always credited.

You Keep the Copyright

All submitted photographs remain entirely your intellectual property. Submission does not transfer copyright or ownership to 61 Kingdom in any form.

Featured on Our Photo Wall

By submitting, you grant 61 Kingdom the right to display your work on our website Photo Wall and in our brand storytelling — always with your name credited.

Non-Commercial Use Only

We pledge never to sell your photos or use them for commercial gain. They are used solely to celebrate and share Kenya's beauty with the world. Digital files will not be returned.
