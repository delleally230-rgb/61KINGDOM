61 Kingdom is a Kenya-based destination management company specialising in incentive travel, luxury tailor-made journeys, and MICE programs across Kenya and East Africa. With over 20 years of professional experience, we combine deep local expertise with international service standards to create travel programs that are seamless, memorable, and professionally executed.

At 61 Kingdom, we believe travel in Africa should go beyond logistics. It should create meaningful connections between people, culture, wildlife, and landscapes. Every journey we design is built on a strong understanding of the destination, local communities, and the expectations of international travellers.

Africa offers extraordinary opportunities for travel, but operating in this dynamic environment requires more than planning — it demands strong local networks, efficient coordination, and the ability to adapt quickly when circumstances change. That is where 61 Kingdom stands apart.

Our direction

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To become the most respected inbound tourism service provider in Kenya and across East Africa — connecting the wonders of Africa with the world through exceptional customer service, sustainable tourism practices, and carefully curated travel experiences.

Our Mission

To connect people with nature and support a sustainable future. We aim to protect the natural environment through internationally minded professional standards, create meaningful travel experiences for guests from around the world, and promote the enduring value of Africa's natural wonders.
